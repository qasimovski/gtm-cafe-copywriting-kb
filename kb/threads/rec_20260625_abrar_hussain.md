**Abrar Hussain** - `cold_email`

> Three variants to founders of B2B AI companies (4-20 headcount). A: credential-led, 'I'll keep this short', PS 'This is a cold email'. B: retainer-churn angle with 500k+ emails/month proof and 5 free meetings. C: very long, self-aware 'chances of you replying are practically nil'.

## Feedback

### Kellen - critique / overall

Email A is closest. Keep the 'founder-led sales starts to cap out' idea. Cut the proof pile, LinkedIn line, and PS. It feels too much like you are trying to establish credibility instead of naming their problem.

### Kellen - critique / personalization

Email B is the wrong audience. It sounds like it was written for agencies with retainer churn, not B2B AI founders. Also '500k+ emails a month' may hurt you because AI founders will worry about spam and market damage.

### Kellen - critique / length

Email C is way too long. The insight is good, but it reads like you are trying to prove you understand every possible pressure they face. Pick one tension only. Keep it around 50 words.

### Kellen - critique / overall

Do not offer '5 free meetings' in the first email. It makes the offer feel cheap and creates skepticism.

### Kellen - critique / cta

Avoid 'quick Loom.' It is overused. Ask to send the specific outbound wedge or test instead.

### Kellen - rewrite / overall

Sample built on the single founder-attention tension.

Suggested replacement:

```
Subject: founder led / Founder-led sales usually works until it starts costing too much founder attention, {{first_name}}. / We help lean AI teams test outbound before hiring sales too early. / The aim is predictable pipeline without pulling more time from product. / Would it help to see the first wedge we would test for {{company_name}}?
```

### Drew Coryer - critique / opening_line

Leading with 'I'll keep this short...' adds unnecessary words (especially if it's not short).

### Drew Coryer - critique / overall

Every line in 1A is framed as 'I', might be worth testing against their situation and saying I less. The framing Kellen shared made it all about the problem, instead of the service you provide.
