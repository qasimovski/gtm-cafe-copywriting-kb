## Feedback

### Ihor Seheda - critique / opening_line

Maybe worth trying to swap the closed yes/no question for something more open-ended, like observation plus soft question.

Suggested replacement:

```
Post-raise is usually when video shifts from 'nice to have' to actually mattering for {{vertical}} companies. Wondering if that's true for {{companyName}} too.
```

### Ihor Seheda - critique / body

The story is 'helped them raise' but you are targeting people who already raised. A different moment for the pitch. Consider reframing the angle for post-raise needs, not pre-raise fundraising.

Suggested replacement:

```
I worked with Solstice, another NY HealthTech startup, to film the hero video on their website. Their founder said it changed how enterprise buyers and candidates perceived them in the months after their $21M raise.
```

### Eddy Okun - general_comment / body

There might be more than one motivation for someone to upgrade their brand collateral. In a world where AI has raised the floor of asset production, the only way to stand out is with bespoke real-world assets that are harder to replicate with AI.
