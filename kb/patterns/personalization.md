# Personalization and targeting

Aggregated from #copywriting-feedback, 2026-05-28 to 2026-08-22. Every line cites the record it came from; open `kb/threads/<id>.md` for full context.

**20 observations from 11 reviewers.** Most active here: Kellen (7), Ihor Seheda (4), John Kitsmiller (1), MattyIce (1), Selim Adi. (1).

## What reviewers flag

- **Ihor Seheda:** The main bottleneck is persona-level specificity, not the idea or tone. You lack one explicit link between your angle and a concrete pressure this SVP actually wakes up worrying about.  `[rec_20260615_muhammad_abbas]`
- **Kellen:** Email B is the wrong audience. It sounds like it was written for agencies with retainer churn, not B2B AI founders. Also '500k+ emails a month' may hurt you because AI founders will worry about spam and market damage.  `[rec_20260625_abrar_hussain]`
- **Kellen:** Targeting lens: 'digital marketing agencies, 11 to 50 employees' is okay, but the angle is still broad. Strong targeting defines market, persona, and angle - not just firmographics.  `[rec_20260629_ali_qureshi]`
- **Kellen:** On scraping their previous podcast appearances to open with: you could, but imo easy to mess up w AI.  `[rec_20260710_mikulas]`
- **Youssef Hesham:** Do a better job on your account discovery & qualification to personalize vs throwing 'Most teams deal with X' - they might not be, and will just skip it if that's the case. Avoid shooting blind darts.  `[rec_20260712_varun_y]`
- **Ihor Seheda:** Segment meaningfulness 2/5 - the real segment (builder-type founders, not sales-led agencies) is hinted at through the pain line but never made an explicit filter.  `[rec_20260729_tim_keen]`
- **Max Pidvalnyi:** You might split test not saying you saw they're using QuickBooks, but just aligning the value proposition with it - it will still sound relevant but less stalkery // spammy.  `[rec_20260810_pyami]`
- **Mikulas:** What role are you sending this to? If your message goes to the CEO they might not live in the day to day to feel that pain of 'stitching 5 spreadsheets'.  `[rec_20260822_anu_biswas]`

  ```
  Does {{analystName}} ever have to pull reports from 5 different places, {{firstName}}? / Is {{analystName}} pulling data from all over the place, or do you have it in one spot?
  ```

- **Ihor Seheda:** F&B ecom brands with lots of tools may not be specific enough to write a sharp message. Decide if you're going after multi-plant manufacturers (waste/yield/downtime pain) vs DTC-first brands (inventory/demand pain).  `[rec_20260822_anu_biswas]`

## Rewrites they actually wrote

- **Kellen:** Provided a second variant for prospects with no founder story, swapping in brand promise.  `[rec_20260529_sana_choudary]`

  ```
  Subject: reviews / {{Company}} seems built around {{brand_promise}}, which makes the lower reviews feel especially useful, {{First_Name}}. / A few 2-star comments often show where the customer expectation starts to break. / Would it be useful to see what showed up for {{Company}}?
  ```


## Stated principles

- **Kellen:** 1:1 doesn't matter. People get excited to buy a Diet Coke or Starbucks coffee every day without personalization. Your iPhone isn't personalized. Empathy, awareness, uniqueness are all underrated concepts.  `[rec_20260604_ben_aperoadvisors_com]`
- **John Kitsmiller:** WIIFM - What's In It For Me. For gov there is almost never a single decision maker so you have to answer the WIIFM for every persona in your messaging.  `[rec_20260604_ben_aperoadvisors_com]`
- **Ihor Seheda:** On referencing that the founder met a team member in London: the more relevant contextual reference the better. Kinda warm intro social proof - shows internal demand and explains why the SVP is the right next step.  `[rec_20260615_muhammad_abbas]`
- **MattyIce:** An AE got a CRO from an AI lab responding after taking a picture of their subway ad and sending it over with 'haha what a sweet ad, saw this on the way home you guys are crushing it'. Sometimes it's just not that deep.  `[rec_20260701_mattyice]`
- **Selim Adi.:** A top-performing AE used to take a photo of corporate offices selfie-style, send a DM about being in the area and ask to chat; it really is that simple.  `[rec_20260701_mattyice]`
- **Kellen:** What sorts of agencies are being targeted? (author admitted he'd just rounded up emails he already had)  `[rec_20260729_tim_keen]`
- **jorge carrillo:** For healthcare providers, the NPI Registry gives you both companies and people. For clinics, Discolike beats Google Maps; for individual doctors Maps is superior.  `[rec_20260729_will_allred]`
- **Jai Toor:** NPI + google maps data. Public + private combo works really well for HC providers.  `[rec_20260729_will_allred]`
- **Will Allred:** Depending on their work history this can be segmented well. Ex. coming from a bigger company - you can talk about matching bigger co maturity w/o the headache.  `[rec_20260811_mick]`
- **Kellen:** I'd do like a 4x matrix and add this as the other dimension. Sometimes more specific isn't better. Sometimes it is. From my experience.  `[rec_20260813_barash]`
