# Opening lines

Aggregated from #copywriting-feedback, 2026-05-28 to 2026-08-22. Every line cites the record it came from; open `kb/threads/<id>.md` for full context.

**15 observations from 5 reviewers.** Most active here: Kellen (10), Ihor Seheda (2), ben - aperoadvisors.com (1), Drew Coryer (1), Adnan Manna (1).

## What reviewers flag

- **Drew Coryer:** Leading with 'I'll keep this short...' adds unnecessary words (especially if it's not short).  `[rec_20260625_abrar_hussain]`
- **Kellen:** Email 1: too much fake warmth, too much certainty. 'Love your work' and 'crushing it' feel templated.  `[rec_20260629_ali_qureshi]`
- **Kellen:** Email 3: strongest hook, weakest trust. Opening with a guarantee screams 'appointment setter.' It may get attention, but likely from lower-quality or skeptical replies.  `[rec_20260629_ali_qureshi]`
- **Kellen:** I would open w the offer first sentence maybe, but agree if this isn't getting volume, infra or list likely issue. Or they just don't want it.  `[rec_20260701_malak]`
- **Adnan Manna:** 'Most teams...' pass. Statements of fact and assumptions don't get replies. See their product's change logs, subreddits complaining about a recent update that broke something, and use that for a first line.  `[rec_20260712_varun_y]`
- **Kellen:** Line 1 email 1 could be clearer - it is an open loop and warrants asking for what? You answer it line 2, but line 1 needs to make sense stand alone to get them to keep going.  `[rec_20260722_ivan]`
- **Kellen:** 'Joining us' is hurting you // makes it more about you imo vs 'are you planning to be in xyz?' which is fully them centric.  `[rec_20260722_ivan]`

  ```
  are you going to be at the xyz in location this weekend, name?
  ```

- **Kellen:** 'saw' as an opener is more basic.  `[rec_20260811_abhi_at_gtmcafe]`
- **Ihor Seheda:** Maybe worth trying to swap the closed yes/no question for something more open-ended, like observation plus soft question.  `[rec_20260819_tim_keen]`

  ```
  Post-raise is usually when video shifts from 'nice to have' to actually mattering for {{vertical}} companies. Wondering if that's true for {{companyName}} too.
  ```

- **Kellen:** Imo opening question too aggro and complicated.  `[rec_20260822_anu_biswas]`

## What reviewers endorse

- **Kellen:** love the opener, i am a fan of 'presumed personalization'. Instead of 'hey name - noticed x' where focus is on x at the end, this subtly lands 'i know youve been ceo for x long' and focuses on the argument you're making. understated personalization ftw.  `[rec_20260616_kyle_nelson]`
- **ben - aperoadvisors.com:** I like that ^^  `[rec_20260616_kyle_nelson]`
- **Ihor Seheda:** Persona in context 4/5 - the 'vibe-coding... don't drive revenue' line is specific and well-targeted, genuinely situational rather than generic.  `[rec_20260729_tim_keen]`

## Rewrites they actually wrote

- **Kellen:** Open on the search-volume number rather than the service.  `[rec_20260706_abhi_at_gtmcafe]`

  ```
  6k searches for 'xyz' happen every month, name
  ```

- **Kellen:** Anchor to what the prospect would choose to do rather than to your own framing.  `[rec_20260729_tim_keen]`

  ```
  If you had 16 uninterrupted hours to focus on any {{company}} project you wanted, what would it be name? / Asking because after selling my own - am hoping to connect with people to support interesting projects. / You might need x, y or z... wondering if it would be useful to connect?
  ```
