## What reviewers flag

- **Kellen:** Make cta better. The thesis is good so make the cta a more direct literal ask aka move the ball forward.  `[rec_20260528_john_richards]`
- **Kellen:** Who tf cares if you're off. You care if he wants to learn more or you care if she wants to see samples of your work.  `[rec_20260528_john_richards]`

  ```
  if you want - I can do a {form type} by hand on video to prove I know my stuff
  ```

- **Kellen:** id ask them something direct tbh  `[rec_20260615_muhammad_abbas]`
- **Kellen:** Avoid 'quick Loom.' It is overused. Ask to send the specific outbound wedge or test instead.  `[rec_20260625_abrar_hussain]`
- **Kellen:** CTA lens: 'Worth a conversation?' and 'Worth 10 minutes?' are meeting asks - too heavy for this much skepticism. Better CTAs start a reply, like 'Would it be useful to see the targeting angle?' Campaign performance often depends on whether the CTA matches the buyer's readiness.  `[rec_20260629_ali_qureshi]`
- **Kellen:** 'Worth a chat?' is easy, but generic. A stronger soft ask should open a specific loop, like comparing outsourced resolved-ticket coverage against the planned hire.  `[rec_20260703_matt_sezgin]`
- **Youssef Hesham:** Have a better offer for them - ideally something they would steal from you if they had access to your platform, vs something that is publicly available already.  `[rec_20260712_varun_y]`
- **Nick:** Have a softer CTA than a quick call, because people are usually skeptical about these offers. Have a document stating how you do it; once they reply, send the doc and then ask for a call.  `[rec_20260801_zargham_saeed]`
- **Mikulas:** Definitely wouldn't recommend sending loom links without people opting in. 'quick call' CTAs perform typically the worst.  `[rec_20260822_anu_biswas]`

## Rewrites they actually wrote

- **Kellen:** Suggested video-ask framing that leads with the question rather than the credential.  `[rec_20260528_john_richards]`

  ```
  hey name - would you watch a video on xyz if I send it to you? Asking because I've helped other blah blah. I work fractionally on xyz. Any interest?
  ```

- **Adnan Manna:** Make the CTA about their specific product.  `[rec_20260712_varun_y]`

  ```
  Would you like to see how it would work for 'their product'?
  ```

- **Kellen:** Make the CTA a concrete effort trade.  `[rec_20260730_abhi_at_gtmcafe]`

  ```
  if I spend the 45 min to map how I'd approach this, will you take a look?
  ```


## Stated principles

- **Kellen:** Complex products can feel like they 'give homework' - hard or long to derive value, thus not enticing. Getting them to say yes to seeing or reading something may work better, or offering a quick 5-min back and forth.  `[rec_20260617_max_pidvalnyi]`
- **Kellen:** even better if the proposal is the finish line vs the reply  `[rec_20260706_abhi_at_gtmcafe]`
- **Preeti:** I call them and say hey you replied to my email, then ask two questions: how do they currently acquire customers, and what are their current challenges with acquisition. That usually leads to them sharing what's going on, then I ask for 15-20 mins tomorrow and walk them through the system.  `[rec_20260715_john_oz]`
- **Kellen:** Also good opportunity to lead w lead magnet or give away.  `[rec_20260810_pyami]`
